\section{Research Interests}
\small{My research interests lie at the intersection of embedded systems, wireless communications, and resource-constrained computing. I have research experience in designing intermittent computing architectures and energy-harvesting mechanisms for battery-free IoT devices, as well as developing efficient firmware for resource-constrained hardware. My work also includes low-power wireless communications, with hands on experience in wake-up radio protocols, UWB, and other wireless technologies. I am particularly interested in hardware-software co-design methodologies for constrained devices, focusing on developing efficient and reliable embedded systems that bridge low-level hardware capabilities with higher-level sensing and communication requirements.}
