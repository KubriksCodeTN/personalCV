\section{Research Interests}
\small{My research interests lie at the intersection of embedded systems, wireless communication, and resource-constrained computing. I have research experience in the design of intermittent computing architectures and energy-harvesting mechanisms for battery-free IoT, as well as in developing efficient firmware for resource-constrained hardware. On the other hand, my work also includes low-power wireless communication, with hands-on experience in wake-up radio protocols, UWB, and other wireless technologies. I am interested in hardware-software co-design methodologies for constrained devices, with a focus on developing efficient and reliable embedded systems that bridge low-level hardware capabilities with higher-level sensing and communication requirements}
